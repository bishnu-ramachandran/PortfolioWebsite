Research

As a Graduate Research Assistant in the Sustainable Materials Lab at UBC (supervisor: Prof. Karthik Akkiraju), I am developing a multiscale, ML-driven framework for scoring absolute sustainability: how a material or process performs relative to planetary boundaries, rather than relative to another product.

Micro

DFT & MLIPs

Atomistic modelling of material properties with machine-learned interatomic potentials.

→
Meso

Life cycle impact analysis

Process-level impacts, linked to the atomistic descriptors.

→
Macro

System dynamics (STELLA)

Normalising descriptors against planetary boundaries and human quality-of-life impact.

I also contribute socio-economic inputs to a lignin valorisation project and mentor undergraduate students on their research projects.
