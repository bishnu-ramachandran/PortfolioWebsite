Research

As a Graduate Research Assistant in the Sustainable Materials Lab at UBC (supervisor: Prof. Karthik Akkiraju), I am developing multiscale, ML-driven frameworks for sustainability modelling.

Scale I

DFT

Atomistic modelling of material properties with machine-learned interatomic potentials.

→
Scale II

Process-level impacts, linked to the atomistic descriptors.

→
Scale III

System Dynamics

Assessment against planetary boundaries and human quality-of-life impact.
